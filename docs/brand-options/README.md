# Simplified icon alternatives

Three alternatives were generated with the built-in image generation tool, in the displayed order below. The user selected **option 3, Little collector**, which is now the application icon and the basis for both empty-state mascots. The other two are retained as design exploration.

1. [Pocket companion](01-pocket-companion.png): a large simple head and a compact clipboard.
2. [Piggy clipboard](02-piggy-clipboard.png): a pig face becomes the top clip of the board.
3. [Little collector](03-little-collector.png): a small pig hugs the clipboard at an angle.

The supplied character artwork was attached as a style reference for simple contours and a minimal face. The pig characters and clipboard compositions are new designs. All three use genuine transparent PNG backgrounds.

The active empty-history animation follows the selected clipboard's tilt: three native strokes appear in order, rest, fade, then repeat; occasional blinks remain. The matching empty pinned list shows the pig pressing a pin into a note. Reduce Motion leaves both mascots still. See [the active brand notes](../brand-design.md).

Entire mascot visible with balanced 10% transparent margins. Product illustration only, no UI/mockup/page or presentation.
```text
Direction name: Pocket companion.
Composition: a large squat round pig HEAD above a small simple ivory clipboard, in a single compact silhouette. This is the most stripped-down friendly face: just two tiny dark vertical oval eyes, one flat coral oval snout with two short nostril marks; no mouth. Two tiny simple triangular ears seamlessly protrude from the top of the head, no folds or inner-ear decoration. No hair, eyebrow or eyelashes. No big individual fingers or elaborate paws: at most two tiny single-arc mittens hug the sides of the clipboard. The ivory clipboard occupies the lower third of the silhouette, with one small flat coral rounded clip and three simple rounded horizontal brown strokes, last shorter. Pig head and clipboard have spacious flat color fields and VERY FEW interior outlines. More mascot avatar than illustration. Simple enough to sketch in a few confident strokes.
```
